What you can find in each profile

Every domain page in AdsDB surfaces three data categories pulled directly from public IAB transparency files.

📄

app-ads.txt & ads.txt Entries

See all DIRECT and RESELLER relationships declared by the publisher, the ad systems and seller IDs involved, and a breakdown by ad network. Spot missing or misconfigured entries at a glance.

🏢

sellers.json Data

View the seller's contact information, declared seller type (PUBLISHER, INTERMEDIARY, or BOTH), and whether the seller is marked as confidential. Cross-reference with app-ads.txt to validate supply chain integrity.

📊

Statistics & Metadata

Each profile includes total row counts, number of unique ad networks, a DIRECT vs RESELLER ratio, and the last-updated timestamp — so you always know how fresh the data is.
